How It Works

From call to completion in six straightforward steps. We handle the details so you don't have to.

1

Consultation

We visit your property, discuss your vision, take measurements, and provide a detailed estimate, free of charge.

2

Design

Select your pattern, colors, and border details. We handle permits and schedule around your timeline.

3

Site Prep

Excavation, grading, compacted sub-base, forms, and reinforcement. The foundation that makes everything last.

4

Pour & Stamp

We pour air-entrained concrete, apply integral color and release agent, then hand-stamp your chosen pattern.

5

Detail & Seal

After curing, we wash, cut control joints, and apply professional-grade sealer for lasting color and protection.

6

Final Walkthrough

We walk the finished project with you, provide care instructions, and ensure your complete satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Massachusetts homeowners ask about stamped concrete.

Stamped concrete typically ranges from $12–$20 per square foot depending on pattern complexity, color choices, and project size. Basic patterns start around $12/sq ft, while premium designs with multiple colors can reach $20+. We provide free detailed estimates.

Absolutely. Properly installed and sealed stamped concrete handles Massachusetts freeze-thaw cycles very well. We use air-entrained mixes and sealers specifically designed for the Northeast. Resealing every 2–3 years keeps it protected.

We prioritize safety by adding a slip-resistant additive to our final high-grade sealer. This creates a "sandpaper" like grip that is invisible to the eye but provides excellent traction for pool decks and walkways, even when wet or icy.

New England winters are tough on standard concrete. To prevent cracking and scaling, we use an air-entrained concrete mix specifically engineered for the Northeast. This specialized mix contains microscopic air bubbles that allow the concrete to expand and contract safely as temperatures fluctuate.

Yes. We are familiar with the local building codes in the 25+ communities we serve, from Andover to Lexington. As part of our simplified 6-step process, we handle the permit application details so you don't have to worry about the paperwork.

Most residential projects take 3–5 days including site prep, pouring, stamping, and initial curing. You can walk on it within 24–48 hours, but wait 5–7 days for heavy furniture or vehicles.

Requirements vary by town. Smaller patios and walkways often don't need permits, but driveways and larger projects usually do. We handle permitting for you and know the codes in every town we serve.

30+ patterns including ashlar slate, flagstone, cobblestone, brick, wood plank, and custom designs. Colors range from natural earth tones to bold accents with integral color and antiquing release agents.
