About Andover

Why Andover Requires Expert-Grade Concrete

Incorporated in 1646, Andover is an affluent community of 36,569 residents in the heart of the Merrimack Valley. Home to Phillips Academy and miles of scenic Shawsheen River trails, this prestigious town demands concrete installations that match both its architectural elegance and its demanding climate.

Harsh Merrimack Valley Winters

Andover averages 48 inches of snow per year with winter lows dropping to 19 degrees F. The town experiences 80+ freeze-thaw cycles between November and April, which is the leading cause of concrete cracking and spalling. Our air-entrained mix designs and 6-inch compacted gravel sub-base are specifically engineered for these punishing conditions.

48 Inches of Annual Precipitation

Andover receives approximately 48 inches of rain annually, with June being the wettest month. Combined with the town's loamy till soils that can drain slowly in low-lying areas near the Shawsheen River and Ballardvale, proper drainage engineering is critical. We grade every installation for positive water runoff and adjust sub-base depth for local conditions.

Historic Estates Demand Premium Aesthetics

From the planned brick-facade homes of Shawsheen Village to the stately Colonials near Phillips Academy, Andover's architectural heritage requires stamped concrete that complements, not competes. We bring physical pattern and color samples to your property so you can see exactly how they look against your home before any work begins.

Loamy Till Soils Require Proper Sub-Base

Andover sits on Canton and Woodbridge series soils, moderately well-drained loamy formations over glacial lodgment till. Properties near the Shawsheen River, Ballardvale, and Haggetts Pond can have high water tables. We evaluate every site and install extended sub-bases where drainage is a concern to prevent frost heaving and settling.

Shawsheen Village

Designed in the early 1900s by the American Woolen Company, Shawsheen Village is one of Andover's most architecturally distinctive neighborhoods. Its planned brick homes and grand entrances call for stamped concrete that honors the era. Ashlar Slate and European Fan in warm gray and sandstone tones are the most requested patterns here.

Ballardvale

A historic village along the Shawsheen River, Ballardvale offers a more rural, wooded feel with properties dating to the 1800s. The higher water table near the river requires careful sub-base engineering. Natural Flagstone and Cobblestone patterns in earth tones are favorites, blending with the neighborhood's scenic, natural character.

Country Club & West Parish

The Andover Country Club area and West Parish feature larger lots with well-maintained homes and expansive landscaping. Italian Slate and custom multi-color patterns are popular choices for this neighborhood's upscale aesthetic.
